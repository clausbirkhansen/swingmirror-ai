Connection & troubleshooting

Ready. Start the camera on one device and connect the viewer on the other.

Mobile hotspots and some cellular networks block direct connections. A TURN relay (see config.js) makes pairing reliable in those cases.

Version 0.9 · Video is processed on your devices. WebRTC uses a signaling service to pair the devices. No cloud video storage is included.
